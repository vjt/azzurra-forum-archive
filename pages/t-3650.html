Hi,
given the increasing activity of spambots, trojans, flooders connecting to Azzurra from foreign countries, we are forced to restrict the round-robin servers' access policies once more. To connect to Azzurra from a foreign country you have to use a specific server based on your geographic location:

All european users, use irc.eu.azzurra.org (type /server irc.eu.azzurra.org on your mIRC status window)

Users from the United States and Canada, use irc.us.azzurra.org

Users from Oceania and Asia, use irc.as.azzurra.org

Users from Africa, use irc.af.azzurra.org.

For those accessing Azzurra via javIRC applet, the following URL is available: http://javachat.azzurra.org. If you want to join a channel automatically, for example #italia, use:

http://javachat.azzurra.org/index.php?channel=italia

(Modify "italia" with your channel's name)

Sorry for the inconvenience.
