Following the request of some US technology companies, the US State Department has launched the Global Internet Freedom Task Force to monitor the restrictions placed on the Internet by government regimes around the world.

Jolette Shiner, one of the people running the task force, said: “I will say that we have very serious concerns about the protection of privacy and data throughout the Internet globally and, in particular, some recent cases raised in China.”

Companies such as Microsoft, Google and Yahoo have come under fire recently with accusations that they have aided in the censoring of the Internet in China and in some cases, led to the arrests of political dissidents.
